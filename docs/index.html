Abstract

We reconstruct the proof of nonvanishing of Dirichlet L-functions in Re s > 1/2, apart from the principal pole. A zero there would obstruct a one-sided estimate for a centered weighted prime sum. Along a chain of theta lifts the remainder from that sum enters the squared norms of short vectors with a negative sign. A comparison with lower bounds for their Gram determinant therefore gives an upper bound for the remainder. The lower estimate uses a completion to a basis and twisted determinants at the finite embeddings.
